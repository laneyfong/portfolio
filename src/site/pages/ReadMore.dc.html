The Lighthouse Keeper’s Daughter

When the last lighthouse on a remote northern coast is scheduled for automation, eleven-year-old Wren refuses to leave the only home she has known. Her father, the keeper, has one winter left before the lamp is switched to a timer and the island is closed for good.

As storms cut them off from the mainland, Wren starts finding notes tucked into the lamp room, written in a hand she doesn’t recognise and dated decades apart. Each one points to a ship the light once guided home.

With the help of a retired radio operator and a very stubborn goat, she pieces together the island’s forgotten history, and a reason the light might still be worth keeping on by hand.
